Need to know

Frequently Asked Questions

Some quick answers before you get in touch.

What types of documents do you accept?

Document formats and size limits depend on the selected service. Contact us first to confirm whether your file can be reviewed.

What does AI Writing Humanization include?

We improve clarity, tone, flow and sentence variety in your draft while preserving meaning and checking for unsupported claims. We do not promise any particular AI-detection score or help misrepresent authorship.

Do you provide a Turnitin AI check?

Turnitin AI detection and similarity checks can be requested, subject to authorized tool access and report availability. Please confirm the specific report and turnaround before ordering. Research Bari is not presented as an official Turnitin partner.

Is a Turnitin AI detection result always accurate?

No. AI-detection tools can produce false positives and false negatives. A detector score is an estimate, not definitive proof that a person did or did not write a text.

What does a Turnitin similarity report show?

A similarity report identifies text matches against sources available to the selected tool. Similarity is not automatically plagiarism; quotations, references and context must be reviewed.

Can you help with a thesis or research paper?

Yes. We support thesis chapters, term papers and research manuscripts through topic development, outlines, literature-search guidance, methodology feedback, editing, formatting and data-analysis guidance. Your research, sources and final submission remain your responsibility.

How do I know the price and delivery time?

Send the service type, approximate word count or page count, file format and deadline through WhatsApp. We will confirm the scope, quote and estimated timeline before work begins.

How do you handle document confidentiality?

Files should only be shared through an agreed channel and used for the requested service. Please avoid sending unnecessary personal or sensitive information.
